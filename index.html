We'd love to welcome you

Visiting a church for the first time can feel daunting. Here's everything you need to know before you arrive.

Service Times
Sunday 10:30 am · Wednesday 7:30 pm
Contact
info@hcf-baptist.co.uk
020 8748 XXXX
Nearest Tube
Ravenscourt Park (District line, 3 min walk)

What should I wear?
Come as you are — genuinely. You'll see everything from smart casual to jeans. We care far more about you being there than what you're wearing.
Is there parking nearby?
Street parking is available on surrounding roads on Sundays. Ravenscourt Park station is a 3-minute walk, and several bus routes stop nearby.
What about my children?
Children are very welcome! We run age-appropriate groups during the Sunday service for ages 0–11 (crèche, Explorers, and Pathfinders), so children and parents can both engage fully.
Do I need to book in advance?
No booking needed for regular services — just turn up! For special events like the Easter Lunch, we may ask you to register so we can plan catering.
