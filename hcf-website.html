Who We Are

More than a Sunday service — a family

Hammersmith Christian Fellowship is a vibrant church community rooted in the love of Christ and the life of our local neighbourhood. Founded under Rev. Alonzo Clarke and currently led by Pastor Keith Barry, we've been serving Hammersmith for decades.

We believe faith should be lived — in worship on Sundays, in community through the week, and in service to our neighbours in West London.

  • Rooted in Scripture, welcoming to all questions and doubts
  • Active in the Hammersmith community all week long
  • Part of the OBU — a wider family of Baptist churches
  • Children and youth programmes every Sunday
  • Small groups, prayer meetings and pastoral care

What We Believe
01
The Bible is the inspired Word of God, our ultimate guide for life and faith.
02
Jesus Christ is Lord — fully God, fully human — who died and rose again for our salvation.
03
Every person is made in God's image and deserving of dignity, love, and welcome.

The church exists to serve God, each other, and the wider community of Hammersmith.
